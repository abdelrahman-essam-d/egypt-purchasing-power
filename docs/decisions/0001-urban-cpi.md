# Use urban CPI as the main series

Date: 2026-10-08

## Context

Questions 1 and 2 ask about the real value of wages (adjusted for inflation), so we need a CPI series. Egypt publishes two: urban and national. We must choose one as the main series.

## Decision

We use urban CPI as the main series, because it is the one published in the news every month, so we can compare our numbers with published ones. We use national CPI as a second check, to see whether our results change much with a different series.

## Alternatives we rejected

- No CPI, only the USD/EGP exchange rate: Questions 1 and 2 need inflation-adjusted values.
- National CPI as the main series: we could not compare it as easily with numbers published in the news.
- Annual CPI only (World Bank): Question 2 needs monthly values.

## Consequences / risks

- Good: we can compare our urban numbers with published ones.
- Not confirmed: "urban is what the news publishes every month" comes from earlier research. We will check it when we reach the CPI step.
- Risk: the monthly values come from PDF bulletins, and extracting numbers from them may be hard. We will test this before building on it.
